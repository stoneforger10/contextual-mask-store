Notes / Description (under 1,000 characters):
```text
ContextualMaskStore is a reusable GenLayer public-text transformation primitive, not a graph, certificate or execution capability. A caller binds a public HTTPS source to its raw SHA-256 hash. Leader and validators independently fetch the document and classify each deterministic email occurrence as CONTACT, EXAMPLE or UNKNOWN. Exact agreement on the complete report is required. CONTACT occurrences are masked; EXAMPLE occurrences are preserved by a deterministic renderer. UNKNOWN or unavailable/mismatched evidence withholds output. Caller-bound attempts, immutable content-addressed artifacts, linked history and output-hash-bound chunk reads prevent silent replacement and replay. StudioNet demonstrations masked one operational-use occurrence while preserving its placeholder, and preserved an example-only document; receipt consensus and stored outputs were checked. These are synthetic public fixtures, not mailbox-identity proofs. GenVM lint, SDK validation and 25 mocked direct tests pass.
```
